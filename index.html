Hello, I’m Vignesh M.

I’m a video editor and motion graphics creator who cares about making every second count—especially the first ten.

At Brototype, I edit YouTube videos and Instagram Reels for career and tech education channels. I use Premiere Pro and After Effects for pacing, motion graphics, captions, and sound, alongside AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Google Flow, and Higgsfield to streamline my workflow.

I started editing in 2024 at Kayteegee Adtech, spent 10 months freelancing, and joined Brototype in 2025. My engineering and software background helps me learn new tools quickly and work with structure.
